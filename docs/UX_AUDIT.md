# UX audit

Walked on 2026-11-21 on a copy of the owner's store (Williamson opened first),
at 1440×900 and 390×844, over every view. The owner's journey: find
competitors → open a channel → see what is breaking out → ideas → script →
export, plus Settings. No console errors and no sideways scroll were found;
the problems are of structure, reading load and dead ends.

Screenshots of the walk before the changes:
`<scratchpad>/ux/before/<view>-<width>.png` (full pages; `-top.png` crops the
first 1500 px of the tall ones).

## What works and is kept

- The Channel screen: logo, name, labelled numbers from the store, outliers
  and newest videos as thumbnails, competitors as clean rows (close to the
  reference).
- Each view has its own address; back, forward and reload keep it.
- Side panels with one full-width action (idea lab, formats, ask).
- No sideways scroll at 390 px, no console errors.
- Numbers are labelled with what they rest on.
