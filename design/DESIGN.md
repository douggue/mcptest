## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-velocity intelligence, spatial network visualization, and tactical clarity. Designed for founders, growth teams, and market intelligence analysts, the platform transforms high-dimensional competitor graphs and live behavioral data into an intuitive, telemetry-inspired command canvas.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Technical Minimalism** with **Futuristic Cyber-Tactility**:
- Deep void backdrops anchored in obsidian, space navy, and stygian coal create an infinite focal depth.
- High-luminance accents in electric cyan and neon sky punctuate the darkness to guide scanning eyes toward breakthroughs, outliers, and radar nodes.
- Crisp glassmorphic plates, hairline border conduits, and subtle outer glows give interfaces the feel of advanced mission-control HUDs rather than traditional enterprise software.
- The emotional tone is precise, secretive, sophisticated, and deeply empowering—conveying intelligence superiority without sensory overload.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for low-light environments, maximizing optical hierarchy through luminescence ratios rather than ambient light reflection.

- **Primary (`#00D2FF`)**: Electric Cyan serves as the active signal color. It powers the main scanner arcs, interactive node pins, primary interactive buttons, and high-priority state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#38BDF8`)**: Sky Neon provides atmospheric modulation for secondary vectors, connective edges in network graphs, and interactive hover feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`)**: Solar Gold is reserved for dynamic velocity metrics, multiplier pills (e.g., viral coefficients like `83x`, `148398x`), and priority alert tags.
- **Neutral (`#05070E` & `#0B0F19`)**: Deep obsidian-navy tones build visual depth across multiple layers—from the boundless bottom canvas to card plates and hovering toolbars.
- **Contrast Ratios**: Body text uses `#F1F5F9` (92% white) and muted labels use `#94A3B8` (Slate-400), satisfying strict contrast compliance across deep space backgrounds.

## Typography

Typography is clean, highly technical, and strictly neutral. The design relies on **Geist** for headlines, reading copy, telemetry readouts, and badge metrics.

- **Data Density Over Ornament**: Strict tracking controls (`-0.03em` for large headers, `+0.08em` for uppercase meta labels) deliver a calibrated aerospace instrumentation feel.
- **Micro Labels**: Subtitles, status indicators, and section eyebrows use `label-caps` in uppercase format with wide letter spacing to distinguish structural dividers from interactive items.
- **Numbers and Metrics**: All numerical counters, network link tallies, and multiplier tags use tabular figures to ensure numbers align smoothly during dynamic animation cycles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column dynamic framework designed around deep spatial canvases:
- **Canvas vs. Flow**: The platform operates in two modes: a full-bleed pan/zoom radar canvas (viewports with floating overlay instruments) and an analytical stream layout (cards, tables, and discovery lists).
- **Margins & Gutters**: Mobile screens maintain compact `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters for thumb-zone precision. Viewports expanding past `1024px` transition to `1.5rem` gutters with a maximum content container width of `1360px` for data readability.
- **Micro-Scale Spacing**: Inner-component rhythm conforms to 4px base multiples (`space-xs: 4px`, `space-sm: 8px`, `space-md: 16px`), ensuring metric badges, profile chips, and radar sliders align without pixel-drift.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids muddy drop-shadows, using instead layered luminescence, glassmorphism, and neon optical diffusions:

- **Level 0 (Infinite Void)**: `#05070E` base canvas, hosting subtle cosmic gradients or radar grid circles (`rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Plates)**: `#0B0F19` with a delicate 1px perimeter border rendered via `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` or `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Glass Controls & Drawers)**: Frosted translucent containers (`rgba(15, 22, 38, 0.72)`) backed by a 16px Gaussian blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Level 3 (HUD Nodes, Pins & Modals)**: Glass panels framed by active electric cyan borders (`rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.4)`) accompanied by an outer ambient corona (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.18)`).
- **Active Scanning Elements**: Rotating radar vectors utilize a radial gradient fading from `#00D2FF` at 35% opacity down to 0% across a sweeping 60-degree conic sector.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** contours combined with geometric circular motifs:
- **Pill Philosophy**: Action buttons, search bars, tooltips, slider thumbs, and analytical metric badges feature fully rounded, organic pill boundaries (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards and Panels**: Structural tiles and flyout sheets use rounded corners of `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px), preserving high tech elegance while balancing the circular nature of avatar nodes and radar rings.
- **Node Avatars & Orbits**: Competitor entities are strict circles framed by illuminated dynamic rings indicating cluster membership, relevance tiers, or threat scores.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action**: Pill button with solid white `#FFFFFF` or vibrant `#00D2FF` fill, paired with dark obsidian text (`#05070E`), semi-bold weight, and an ambient outer glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Control**: Semi-transparent dark pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), `#F1F5F9` text, and cyan glow upon hover.
- **Icon Action Buttons**: Circular (36px × 36px) or rounded square (40px × 40px with 12px corners) glass containers hosting clean monochrome stroke iconography.

### Multiplier & Metric Badges
- **Viral / Growth Badges**: Compact pill tokens styled with amber backdrops (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), crisp borders (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`), and `#FBBF24` bold typography (e.g., `323533x`).
- **Telemetry Counter**: Pill tag in electric sky cyan with an integrated dot light indicating active streaming status.

### Form Inputs & Query Fields
- **Search & URL Ingestors**: Expansive pill-shaped capsules with deep background fill (`#0B0F19`), inset padding (`12px 20px`), crisp hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and prominent glowing borders (`#00D2FF`) during focus. Placeholders render in subdued slate.

### Radar & Node Graph Visuals
- **Concentric Radar Shells**: Rendered with hairline dashed arcs (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`).
- **Competitor Nodes**: Circular profile avatars encased in dual-ring rims. Active nodes show an outer breathing cyan glow (`0 0 12px #00D2FF`). Connecting network vectors scale in line weight and opacity to represent link strength.

### Content Cards & Intelligence Feeds
- **Breakout Media Row**: Horizontal layout with rounded 16:9 thumbnail previews on the left (complete with high-contrast duration pills in the lower right) and clean typographic metadata, title, and subscriber tags aligned to the right.
- **Bottom Glass Dock**: Floating navigation pill docked along the bottom viewport with frosted backdrop blur and icon-driven routing.
